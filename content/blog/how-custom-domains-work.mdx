## Direct answer

A **custom domain** points your hostname (for example `app.example.com`) at a deployment platform so visitors reach your app on your brand domain instead of the platform’s default hostname. On Runex, create a DNS **CNAME** to `cname.runex.cloud`, then attach the domain to a deployment in the dashboard.

## Platform URL vs custom domain

| | Platform URL | Custom domain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Example | `https://my-app.runex.cloud` | `https://app.example.com` |
| DNS you manage | None | CNAME (or apex alternative) |
| Typical target | n/a | `cname.runex.cloud` |

## How DNS fits in

1. Your deployment already exists on the platform.
2. You create a CNAME at your DNS provider.
3. The platform associates that hostname with your deployment.
4. HTTPS is provisioned for the hostname when the platform supports it (Runex provides HTTPS for supported public deployments).

### Example record

```text
Type:  CNAME
Name:  app
Value: cname.runex.cloud
```

## Apex domains

Some DNS providers cannot place a CNAME on the zone apex (`example.com`). Options usually include ALIAS/ANAME/CNAME flattening — follow your DNS provider’s guidance, or use a subdomain like `www` or `app`.

## Troubleshooting

- **NXDOMAIN / wrong target:** confirm the CNAME value is exactly `cname.runex.cloud`.
- **Stale DNS:** wait for TTL; check with `dig` or your provider’s DNS tools.
- **HTTPS pending:** domain must be attached in Runex after DNS is correct.
